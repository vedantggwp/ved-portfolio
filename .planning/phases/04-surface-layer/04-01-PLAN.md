  <name>Task 1: Select provocation font and copy</name>
  <decision>Choose the distinctive font for the provocation text and the actual two-line couplet copy</decision>
  <context>
The provocation font must NOT be DM Serif Display or Inter. It must match "Venus in Virgo precision," Villeneuve cinematography, and Teenage Engineering aesthetic. The copy must be a two-line couplet -- cryptic/philosophical, second-person voice, uniquely Ved.

### Font Options

**Option A: Cormorant (Light 300)**
High-contrast display Garamond with extreme refinement. Hairline strokes feel like precision instruments. The Light weight is almost dangerously thin -- commands attention through restraint. Strongest match for precision/cinematic brief. Risk: hairline strokes may anti-alias poorly on low-DPI.

**Option B: Instrument Serif (Regular 400)**
Condensed, confident, modern display serif designed for headlines. Clean without being sterile, distinctive without being decorative. Teenage Engineering aesthetic -- functional precision, no ornamentation. Safest bet overall. Single weight only.

**Option C: Libre Caslon Display (Regular 400)**
Display Caslon inspired by 1960s hand-lettered American Caslons. "Discovered, not designed" quality. Warmer and more characterful than standard Caslon revivals. Risk: vintage-modern character could be too warm for precision aesthetic.
