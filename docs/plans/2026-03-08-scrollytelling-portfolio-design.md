# Ved Portfolio — 3D Scrollytelling Design

**Date:** 2026-03-08
**Purpose:** Personal portfolio website for job hunting + personal brand as a developer/builder
**Core concept:** Depth-based scrollytelling with a recurring monolith motif

---

## Design Philosophy

**Core tension:** Stillness on the surface, intensity underneath. Gravitas + momentum.

**What this is NOT:**
- A dark-mode template with "Hi, I'm Ved. I build things." and floating particles
- A tech-stack grocery list
- Corporate polish without soul
- Constellation/network graph cliche

**What this IS:**
- Villeneuve cinematography translated to web
- Dark warmth with sharp edges
- A site that makes people slow down, then lean in
- Proof of thinking, not a list of shipping

---

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette
- **Primary black:** Deep, warm black (not pure #000) — `#0A0A0A` to `#121210`
- **Warm ivory:** Off-white with warmth — `#F5F0E8`
- **Amber/gold accent:** Earned, not decorative — `#C4964A`
- **Muted teal:** Secondary — `#2A6B6B`
- **Deep navy:** Secondary — `#1A1A3E`
- No neon. No pastels. No SaaS gradients.

### Typography
- **Headers:** Editorial serif with character (Playfair Display, DM Serif Display, or similar)
- **Body:** Clean sans-serif (Inter, Satoshi, or similar)
- **Vibe:** "Someone who reads widely and builds precisely"

### Texture
- Not sterile minimalism — minimalism with a pulse
- Geological textures in deep layers (basalt, obsidian with warm veins)
- Subtle grain/noise for depth and warmth
- Single warm light source aesthetic

---

## The Monolith

A recurring geometric form that evolves through the site. The visual through-line.

### Evolution Arc
1. **Surface:** Abstract, minimal, matte black against slightly-less-black. Catches a single warm light. Rotates imperceptibly. Almost unsettling.
2. **Mid-depth:** Reappears in each depth pocket with more facets and internal light. Different geometry per pocket.
3. **Deep:** Fully evolved — complex geometry, internal light as structured patterns. But NOT fully resolved. Still suggests geometry you can't quite see. A system that rewards continued attention, not a puzzle that resolves neatly.

## Site Architecture

### Layer 1: Surface — The Provocation

**What you see:** Black. Deep water texture, barely perceptible. The monolith fades in with a single warm light source.

**Typography:** One line. Editorial serif, warm ivory, letter-spaced wide. Not a bio — a provocation. Something that could only come from Ved. (Copy TBD — must be uniquely his, not generic.)

**No name. No title. No CTA.** Just a question planted in the visitor's head.

**Scroll behavior:** Camera moves *forward*, into the monolith. Surface fractures, amber light bleeds through cracks.

---

### Transition 1: Surface → Mid-Depth

- Monolith surface dissolves into particles, reforms as mid-depth environment
- Color temperature shifts: pure black → deep charcoal with warm undertones
- Single piano note with long reverb (Frahm-style) marks the crossing
- Scroll momentum *resists* briefly — pushing through a membrane — then releases
- The first "ears popping" moment

---

### Layer 2: Mid-Depth — "How I Think"

**Structure:** Three depth pockets. Each is a compressed case study showing cross-domain pattern recognition.

Each pocket has:
- Its own color shift within the palette
- Its own scroll physics (different weight, not just different color)
- Its own sonic texture
- The monolith reappearing in evolved form

#### Pocket 1: The Logic Chain (Teal-Black, Fluid Physics)
**The pattern:** Copy → strategy → tech is not a career pivot. It's the same skill applied at increasing resolution.
**Direction:** "Copywriting taught me that every system has a narrative. Agencies taught me that narratives are systems. AI taught me to build both at once."
**Scroll feel:** Fluid, flowing — ideas connecting naturally

#### Pocket 2: The Invisible Intersection (Amber-Black, Dense Physics)
**The pattern:** Combining accessibility auditing and neuromarketing — two fields nobody was putting in the same room.
**Direction:** Inclusive design and persuasive design are the same problem viewed from different angles. NeuroEdge was built on that insight.
**Scroll feel:** Dense, weighty — the gravity of an insight that changes a field

#### Pocket 3: The Pre-Solved Problem (Navy-Black, Still Physics)
**The pattern:** A 12-hour build isn't fast. It's what happens when you've already solved the problem three times in different domains. The code is just the last translation.
**Direction:** Speed as symptom of deep cross-domain thinking, not hustle.
**Scroll feel:** Still, almost suspended — the calm of certainty

---

### Transition 2: Mid-Depth → Deep

- Heavier than Transition 1. Stronger scroll resistance.
- Ambient tone drops to lower register, more resonance
- Monolith's internal light intensifies
- Background texture shifts to geological: basalt/obsidian with warm amber veins
- Entering the core

### Layer 3: Deep — "What I Built"

Each project is a **discovery**, not a card. Environment shifts around you as you enter each project.

#### Per-Project Structure
1. **The frame:** "Here's the problem nobody else framed this way" — one provocative sentence
2. **The build:** What it is, how it works — concise, visual (browser mockup or short clip)
3. **The proof:** What it demonstrated — metric, outcome, or insight

#### Featured Projects

**NeuroEdge** — Accessibility auditing meets neuromarketing. The insight that inclusive design and persuasive design are the same problem. Frame: "Everyone audits what's broken. Nobody was asking what's invisible."

**Springpod Discovery Simulator** — Gamified client-interview simulator. Frame: "I'd already solved requirements discovery as a strategist, as a copywriter, and as a consultant. The code was the fourth translation."

**FraudShieldAI** — Explainable AI fraud detection for SMBs. Frame: "Enterprise fraud detection is a solved problem. Making it legible to a business owner who isn't a data scientist — that's the actual problem."

**Scrollwise** — Personal reading infrastructure. Frame: "You save hundreds of bookmarks, highlights, and posts across apps. They die in a folder. Scrollwise turns your saved knowledge into a feed you actually revisit — TikTok for your own thinking."

## Sonic Design

**Philosophy:** Jóhannsson, not Zimmer. Not music — atmosphere. Sound as reward for paying attention, not a requirement.

### Per-Layer Sonic Identity
- **Surface:** Barely-audible sub-bass hum. Felt more than heard. Something large running behind walls.
- **Transition 1:** Single piano note, long reverb. Pitch shift (semitone up). The "ears popping."
- **Pocket 1 (teal):** Soft granular hum — fluid, flowing
- **Pocket 2 (amber):** Crystalline shimmer — dense, refracting
- **Pocket 3 (navy):** Low rhythmic pulse — still, measured
- **Transition 2:** Lower register drop. More resonance. Weight.
- **Deep layer:** Individual textures combine into cohesive ambient layer. Harmonic structure emerges. Resolution.
- **Floor:** Long fade to silence.

### Sound Toggle
- Small waveform icon, pulsing gently in corner
- An invitation, not a permission request
- If tapped: the world gains a new dimension
- If ignored: everything still works completely
- No popup, no modal, no overlay

## Technical Architecture

### Stack
- **Framework:** Next.js 15 (App Router)
- **3D Engine:** React Three Fiber (R3F) + Three.js
- **Scroll Engine:** Lenis (smooth scroll) + GSAP ScrollTrigger (pinning, transitions)
- **Audio:** Tone.js for generative micro-interactions OR pre-rendered ambient loops with Web Audio API crossfading
- **Shaders:** Custom GLSL for monolith surface effects, depth transitions
- **Hosting:** Vercel

### Performance Strategy
- Progressive loading per depth layer
- Monolith geometry LOD (level of detail) — reduces on mobile
- **Mobile fallback:** 2D parallax depth with CSS for low-power devices. Still communicates the depth metaphor, just without WebGL.
- Lazy-load 3D scenes as user approaches each layer
- Target: Lighthouse performance 90+ on desktop

### Scroll Physics
- Lenis base with custom dampening curves per section
- Transition zones: scroll-speed reduction (the "membrane" effect)
- Per-pocket physics: different scroll speed multipliers
  - Teal (fluid): 1.0x — natural, flowing
  - Amber (dense): 0.7x — heavier, deliberate
  - Navy (still): 0.5x — near-suspended, contemplative
- GSAP ScrollTrigger for pin-and-release at each depth threshold

### 3D Approach
- Geometric primitives, not photorealistic scenes
- Good lighting + custom shaders over complex models
- Monolith: parametric geometry that morphs via uniforms
- Depth environments: fog, volumetric light approximations, particle reformation
- Keep draw calls low — aesthetic through light and material, not polygon count
